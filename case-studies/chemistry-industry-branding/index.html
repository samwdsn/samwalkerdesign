FINDING THE CHEMISTRY BEHIND THE INDUSTRY.

Chemistry + Industry helps growing businesses with operations management. Its identity needed to inspire confidence in its services whilst remaining approachable and unique to start ups and established businesses.

The brief called for a contemporary direction, with room to express the brand's personality beyond its corporate nature.

A BONDED VISUAL LANGUAGE.

I crafted the identity around the brand's tagline, “the chemistry behind the industry”, leaning into the ideas of chemistry and molecular bonds.

This concept was used throughout the logo suite, iconography and supporting graphics. All of which were wrapped up in a brand book which gave direction across social content, printed collateral and web.
